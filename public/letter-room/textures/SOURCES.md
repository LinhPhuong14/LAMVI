# Texture sources

- `paper-*.webp`: ambientCG **Paper 001**, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper001 — CC0 1.0, https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/. Download `Paper001_1K-JPG.zip`; Color, NormalGL, Roughness maps resized to 512 px and converted to WebP quality 88.
- `wood-*.webp`: Poly Haven **Wood Table**, https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table — CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/license. 1K JPG Diffuse, nor_gl, Rough maps resized to 512 px and converted to WebP quality 88. Color map saturation reduced to .55, contrast to .45 and brightness raised to 1.3 for the warm illustrated palette.

Downloaded 2026-11-13. Normal/roughness maps stay in linear color space; color maps use sRGB. Maps are local assets; no external requests during recipient playback. Procedural material remains the fallback if a local texture cannot load. Paper 001 is generic paper, not a scan of the real LAMVI dó lampshade; fine procedural fibres remain on the lamp.
